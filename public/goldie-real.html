How to get your Printify token
Get your Printify token step by step
First, make sure you are in the right Printify accountSign in to the account that contains the Etsy shop and template products you want Goldie to use. The token connects the whole account. Your template in Step 2 tells Goldie which exact shop to use.
  1. Open Printify and click your profile icon.
  2. Choose My Profile, then open Connections.
  3. If Printify asks for a developer contact email, enter an email you check and save it.
  4. Find Personal Access Tokens and click Generate.
  5. Name it Goldie Listing Factory.
  6. Turn on shops.read, catalog.read, products.read, products.write, uploads.read, uploads.write, and print_providers.read. Goldie does not need order permissions.
  7. Click Generate token, then copy it immediately. Printify only shows the full token once.
  8. Come back here, paste it into the box, and click Connect securely. Goldie will verify the account before you continue.
