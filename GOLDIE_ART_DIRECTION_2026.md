# Goldie Art Direction 2026

## Position

Goldie is an operating system for serious Etsy sellers. It should feel current and desirable, but its first signal must be competence.

## Reference principles

- Rhode: edited assortment, simple language, restrained palette, product confidence.
- SKIMS: solutions-first positioning, warm neutrals, strong typography, minimal ornament.
- Linear: calm density, strict components, clear state, fast navigation.

## Measurable system

- Color allocation: 70% neutral foundation, 20% structural dark contrast, 10% maximum signature accent.
- Spacing: 8-point base. Major gaps use 24, 32, 48, or 64 pixels. Controls use 8, 12, or 16 pixels internally.
- Type: 16-pixel readable body where space allows, 14-pixel minimum instructional text, 12-pixel minimum metadata.
- Contrast: WCAG AA targets for all normal text. Disabled text remains readable and is identified by state, not low opacity alone.
- Actions: one primary action per decision area. Secondary actions never compete with it.
- Radius: 10 to 16 pixels. Avoid inflated pill shapes except for compact status.
- Motion: 160 to 240 milliseconds for feedback. No ambient decoration beyond the current-step pulse.

## Palette

- Carbon: #111113
- Ink: #18171a
- Graphite: #29272c
- Warm stone: #e5e2df
- Soft stone: #f3f1ef
- Muted text: #6c6870
- Aubergine: #65465f
- Dusty lilac: #a990a5
- Signal red: #a43b35

Lilac is a signal, not a background theme. Pink is not a surface color.

## Voice

- Direct, calm, specific.
- Lead with the outcome or the required action.
- Prefer one clear sentence to two reassuring ones.
- Never describe Goldie as magical, seamless, powerful, or intelligent.
- Never use em dashes.
- Avoid narrating what the interface is visibly doing.

## Design test

Every screen must answer, in order:

1. Where am I?
2. What is already done?
3. What do I need to decide?
4. What happens when I continue?
